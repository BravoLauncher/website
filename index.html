Every surface is a button

Back and side taps are read from the accelerometer — not the microphone, not the camera — with filtering tuned on real-world recordings to tell deliberate taps from bumps, drops and table knocks. Every fired tap confirms itself: a short buzz, and for toggles you can't see — auto-rotate, sound, flashlight, mute — a small pill at the top of the screen tells you which state you landed in. Screen taps and gestures come on top.

[ back·tap ]

Back tap — double & triple

Tap twice or three times on the back cover. Works with a case, works while other apps are open, with self-tuning sensitivity and false-positive filtering.

[ side·tap ]

Side tap — double & triple

Tap the edge of the phone instead — easier one-handed, and completely separate from the back gesture, so you get four extra shortcuts in total.

[ screen·tap ]

Screen taps & gestures

Double- or triple-tap empty space on the home screen — or go further with swipes, pinches, two-finger gestures and long-press. Fourteen remappable slots in all.

How to tap & what to expect

Tap with your fingertip — crisp, short taps with the same finger at even strength. It takes a little getting used to; once you find the rhythm, taps should work reliably in everyday use. It isn't perfect: an occasional missed tap or false trigger can happen.

Want to help improve detection? Turn on Settings → Debug → Tap trace logging in the app, reproduce the problem, export the capture and attach it to an issue on github.com/BravoLauncher/BravoLauncher. Traces contain motion-sensor data and basic device details only — no personal information.

Everything a launcher should be

Fast, Material You native, and customizable down to the pixel — without ever phoning home.

01 / gestures

Any tap, any action

Flashlight, lock screen, notification shade, search, recents, mute, auto-rotate, any app — every tap and gesture slot can trigger whatever you choose, with vibration and an on-screen confirmation so you know it fired. Detection is tuned per phone, including older models whose own vibration would otherwise drown out the next tap.

02 / home

Your grid, your rules

Custom rows and columns, page effects, infinite scroll, auto-arrange, icon labels, padding — arranged exactly how you want it.

03 / drawer

A drawer that keeps up

Grid, list or card layout, custom tabs, alphabet fast-scroll, and sorting by name, frequency or most recent. Or switch on the Organiser and get App Library style groups by category or icon colour, worked out entirely on the phone.

04 / theming

Material You, everywhere

Dynamic colors from your wallpaper, monochrome themed icons, icon packs, custom icon shapes, per-app icons and labels, scheduled dark mode.

05 / search

Search anything, ask anyone

Find any app instantly, type math straight into the search bar, or hand your query to your browser — Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, or AI search with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity.

06 / privacy

Hidden & locked apps

Hide apps from the drawer and lock sensitive ones behind your fingerprint or face unlock.

07 / dock

Multi-page dock & folders

A swipeable dock, plus folders with grid, stack, fan or cover previews, configurable open animations and your own label colour.

08 / badges

Notification badges

Dots or counters, positioned and colored your way — reading only the number of notifications, never their content.

09 / more

Widgets, work profile, settings search

Full widget support with resizing, landscape and foldable layouts, work-profile apps handled properly — and a settings search that jumps you straight to any option.

10 / themes

Themes you can share

Export your whole look — grid, dock, drawer, icon style and where every icon, folder and widget sits — as a small file, send it to a friend, and import theirs. Preview any theme live on your own home screen with Keep and Revert, let missing apps be filled with the same kind of app you already have, and bring widgets across when your phone has them. Nothing is downloaded: a theme only arrives as a file you open. On a fresh install the home screen asks which starting look you want, so day one already feels like yours.

11 / backups

Backups that happen by themselves

Every time you finish a round of changes — moving icons, making folders, flipping settings — the launcher quietly writes a full backup a moment later. Keep the last 5 to 50 of them, or switch it off. Manual backups sit in the same list, never count against the limit, and every entry has its own Restore button, so undoing a layout you regret is one tap. Backups are plain files: keep them inside the app, or pick a folder used only for Bravo backups and let your own sync app carry them wherever you like.

12 / on-device

Nothing leaves the phone

Backups, themes, the drawer Organiser's categories, tap detection — all of it is worked out and stored on the device. The app has no networking code, so there is nothing to phone home with, and the built-in settings search means you never need a manual.

Speaks your language

Bravo Launcher is fully translated into 19 languages — every setting, every gesture, even settings search works in your language. Arabic gets proper right-to-left layout, and on Android 13+ you can give Bravo its own language, separate from the rest of your phone.

Your home screen is nobody's business

Bravo Launcher contains no analytics, no ad SDKs and no crash reporters. It makes no network connections — your app list, usage patterns and settings never leave your device. It started as a launcher the developer built for their own privacy, and it ships exactly that way.
